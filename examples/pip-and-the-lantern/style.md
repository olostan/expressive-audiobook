**Genre:** Children's bedtime story

**Tone:** Warm, cozy, soothing, and gently adventurous

Pip, a gentle little fox living alone on the edge of Willow Wood, takes in a wet and lost bird named Wren during a storm. Using her grandfather's old lantern, Pip guides Wren across the river stepping stones back to her waiting family. The story is simple, sensory, and comforting, centering on themes of kindness, bravery, and newfound companionship.

**Recurring Speakers & Vocal Profiles:**

- **Pip:** A kind-hearted, thoughtful young fox who lives alone in a cosy den and eagerly steps up to help a stranger. *Vocal cue: Sweet, bright, earnest, and youthful with gentle enthusiasm.*
- **Wren:** A delicate, cold little bird who is lost and shivering but deeply appreciative. *Vocal cue: Soft, high-pitched, delicate, and slightly breathy, warming into joyful gratitude.*

### Performance & Pacing Directives

1. **Cosy den ambiance** *(Opening of Chapter 1 during the storm and fireside scenes)*
   Emphasize warmth and comfort in the low-to-mid vocal register. Elongate soft vowels slightly when describing the rain tapping on leaves and the sipping of hot acorn tea, creating an inviting, safe atmosphere.

2. **The unexpected knock** *(Chapter 1 when 'Tap, tap, tap' occurs)*
   Drop down to a hushed, curious whisper. Deliver the knocks distinctly with rhythmic staccato pauses between each tap, mimicking an actual timid visitor at a door.

3. **River crossing suspense and relief** *(Chapter 2 during the stepping stones scene and the 'Splash!')*
   Tighten the delivery slightly to reflect focus and caution as they step across the water. Give 'Splash!' a sudden buoyant bounce without sounding terrifying, instantly resolving into Pip's lighthearted giggle and calm forward steps.

4. **Joyful reunion and peaceful conclusion** *(Chapter 2 from Wren's family cheering to the end)*
   Shift into a brighter, celebratory chirp for Wren's family, then slow down noticeably for Pip's journey home. Let the final sentence linger with deep, tranquil satisfaction, trailing off into a soothing sleep-friendly cadence.

Because of the brief duration (under 400 words), maintain a relaxed, unhurried pace throughout to prevent the story from rushing by in under two minutes.
